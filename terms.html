4. Payment Terms

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, RENOVA's standard payment structure is as follows:

  1. 30% Advance — Payable upon signing of the Project Contract. Work commences only after receipt of this payment.
  2. 40% Milestone Payment — Due upon completion of the mid-project milestone as specified in the contract (typically upon completion of primer and preparation works).
  3. 30% Final Payment — Due upon satisfactory completion and project handover, subject to the Client's walkthrough approval.

Accepted payment methods: NEFT/RTGS, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), account payee cheque, and cash (up to ₹2,00,000 per project per Indian IT regulations). A GST-compliant invoice (18% GST on services) will be issued for every payment received.

Payments delayed beyond 7 days of due date shall attract a late payment charge of 1.5% per month on the outstanding amount.

5. Warranties & Guarantees

RENOVA provides the following workmanship warranties on all completed projects:

  • Interior Painting (Basic): 1-year workmanship warranty
  • Interior Painting (Premium): 2-year workmanship warranty
  • Exterior Painting: 5-year weathering warranty (subject to use of approved weathershield products)
  • Texture Finishes: 3-year workmanship warranty
  • Waterproofing: 10-year material warranty (manufacturer) + 2-year workmanship warranty
  • False Ceiling: 2-year structural warranty

Warranties cover defects arising from workmanship errors only. They do not cover damage arising from: structural movement, water ingress from external sources beyond the treated area, impact damage, acts of nature, Client modifications, or use of non-approved cleaning products.

Paint material warranties are provided by the respective manufacturers (Asian Paints, Berger, Nerolac, Dulux, Nippon) and are separate from RENOVA's workmanship warranty.
