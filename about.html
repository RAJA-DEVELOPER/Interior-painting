01 / Our Story

Founded 2012

From a Single Brush
to Mumbai's Most
Trusted Name

RENOVA began in 2012 when Rajiv Menon — a young civil engineer with an obsession for beautiful spaces — completed his first apartment renovation in Andheri. Armed with premium materials, obsessive attention to detail, and a small team of skilled painters, word spread fast.

Today, RENOVA is Mumbai's most sought-after painting and renovation brand, trusted by homeowners, architects, interior designers and corporate clients alike. Over 4,200 projects. Zero compromises.
