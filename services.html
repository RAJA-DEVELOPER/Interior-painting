FAQ

Answers to Your
Questions

A standard 2BHK interior paint job typically takes 5–7 working days, including surface preparation, primer, two coats of paint and final cleanup. Texture finishes or premium finishes may take slightly longer. We always provide a timeline in writing before starting.
For most interior painting jobs, you don't need to vacate. We work room by room and use low-VOC, minimal-odour paints. We cover all furniture and flooring carefully. However, for texture finishes or extensive waterproofing, we recommend staying elsewhere for 1–2 days.
We are authorised applicators for Asian Paints, Berger, Nerolac, Dulux and Nippon. We recommend the best brand for each specific application. You can also specify your preferred brand. All materials come with manufacturer guarantees on top of our workmanship warranty.
Absolutely. Our site visits are completely free, with zero obligation. Our expert will visit your property, assess the scope of work, recommend finishes, and provide a detailed itemised estimate — all at no cost to you.
Yes. All our work comes with a workmanship warranty — 1 year for basic paint, 2 years for premium paint, and up to 5 years for texture finishes and waterproofing. If any defect arises due to our workmanship, we fix it at no extra charge.
We typically require 30% advance on contract signing, 40% at mid-project milestone, and the remaining 30% on satisfactory completion and your approval. We accept NEFT/RTGS, UPI, cheque and cash. A detailed invoice with GST is provided for every payment.
